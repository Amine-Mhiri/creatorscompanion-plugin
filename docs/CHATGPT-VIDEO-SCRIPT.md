# ChatGPT submission: video walkthrough script

OpenAI asks for a short screen recording of the app working inside ChatGPT (the `demo_recording_url` in
the submission). Record once, 2 to 4 minutes, no voiceover needed. Upload as an unlisted YouTube or Loom
link and paste it into the submission form.

## Before recording

- ChatGPT in a desktop browser, signed in with your account, developer mode available (Plugins > Add).
- Remove any older "CreatorsCompanion" test connectors first, so only the one you add appears.
- In another tab, be signed out of creatorscompanion.app (or ready to click "Use a different account").
- Reviewer account: email `reviewer@creatorscompanion.app`, password in `cc-credits/ops/.reviewer-account`.
- Window around 1280 x 800, browser zoom 100%, notifications off.

## Scenes

1. **Add the app (20 s).** Plugins > Add > Add custom MCP server. Name `CreatorsCompanion`, URL
   `https://www.creatorscompanion.app/mcp/chatgpt`, Authentication OAuth, tick the risk box, Create.
2. **Connect (30 s).** Click Connect CreatorsCompanion. On the CreatorsCompanion page: sign in with the
   reviewer email and password (or "Use a different account" first), then "Allow access for chatgpt.com".
   Back in ChatGPT the app shows as connected.
3. **Get started (20 s).** New chat, type `@CreatorsCompanion Help me get started`. Show the answer
   (plan, credits, what is set up, next steps).
4. **Write in my voice (60 s).** `@CreatorsCompanion Write a Reels script in my voice about why remote
   workers lose focus after lunch.` Wait for the draft card. Click a different hook, then "Save to library".
5. **Library (15 s).** `What is in my library?` Show the saved item listed.
6. **Profile update (20 s).** `Update my target audience to remote team leads.` Show the confirmation.
7. **Voice (15 s).** `Show my voice examples for Reels.`
8. **A refusal (15 s, optional).** `What's the weather in Paris?` ChatGPT answers without using the app.

## After recording

- Undo scene 6 so the reviewer account stays as seeded: `Update my target audience back to remote employees
  and freelancers aged 25 to 45 who feel busy.`
- Paste the link in the form; keep the reviewer credentials only in the secure credentials field.
